Paid Tabletop Game Master

Advanced Dungeons & Dragons - Calgary

My cousin taught me to play Basic Dungeons & Dragons when I was eleven and gave me a blue boxed set with a dragon on the cover. Around the same time, my aunt gave me a boxed set of The Lord of the Rings for Christmas, which I devoured. Fantasy fiction and tabletop roleplaying became early creative influences.

  • Secured a paid summer engagement through a job board while in high school, introducing two parents and their son and daughter to Advanced Dungeons & Dragons.
  • Ran the family's campaign using an outline supplied by the father, turning it into sessions, encounters, character decisions, and consequences that responded to the players.
  • Taught the game as we played, explaining rules and helping new players take part in collaborative storytelling and make choices for their characters.
  • Facilitated the parents' use of roleplaying to explore life lessons with their children, including conflicts between the father's thief and the son's paladin.
  • Received a bonus at the end of the engagement and handed the campaign over to the father when school resumed so the family could continue playing.
